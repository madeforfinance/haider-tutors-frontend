Inquiries & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, candid answers regarding our institutional policies, vetting standards, and tuition management protocols.

How does submitting a tuition request work?

expand_more

When a parent or student submits a requirement via our form, our academic intake committee analyzes the curriculum specifications, grade level, schedule requirements, and location constraints. We then notify qualified educators in our network who possess verified credentials in that specific subject and syllabus, facilitating a structured trial session.

Is Haider Tutors an open tutor directory or public marketplace?

expand_more

No. Haider Tutors is not an open directory or ad listing board. We do not display public tutor profiles, ratings, or bidding wars. We operate as a structured academic facilitation platform where all interactions, educator assignments, and student requirements are mediated with institutional quality control.

What qualifications do tutors need to register?

expand_more

Tutors must possess at minimum a relevant university undergraduate degree from an accredited institution, demonstrated mastery of specific exam board syllabi (such as Cambridge, Edexcel, IB, or FBISE), verifiable teaching or tutoring references, and must successfully complete our academic interview and pedagogical assessment.

Which curriculum boards do you support?

expand_more

We provide comprehensive subject coverage across Cambridge International (CAIE O-Levels & A-Levels), Pearson Edexcel, International Baccalaureate (IB MYP & DP), College Board Advanced Placement (AP), US Common Core, Canadian Provincial Syllabi (including OSSD), and Pakistani Federal & Provincial Boards (FBISE, Punjab, Sindh).

How do online lessons take place?

expand_more

Virtual lessons occur within encrypted interactive classrooms featuring digital whiteboard technology, real-time problem-solving with graphics tablet inputs, past-paper annotation, and recorded summaries for continuous student revision.

Can I request in-person home tuition outside Pakistan?

expand_more

In-person home tuition is primarily concentrated across urban centers in Pakistan (Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi) and select partner districts in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. For all other international territories (including US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Japan), we exclusively deliver 1-on-1 virtual instruction.

Privacy Policy

Haider Tutors collects only the details you submit — tuition requirements, academic information and contact details — to coordinate tutor matching and scheduling.

Parent and student contact details are never shown in tutor-facing tuition listings. Personal contacts are withheld until a placement is confirmed by the academic coordination committee.

Account details are used solely for portal access, placement updates and coordination messages. We do not sell personal information.

Terms & Conditions

Tuition placements are coordinated by the Haider Tutors academic committee. Tutor profiles are credential-verified before any family introduction.

Fees are confirmed at placement time and coordinated through the platform. Schedules are agreed between the family and the assigned tutor before sessions begin.

Either party may end a placement with reasonable notice; the coordination team will arrange a suitable replacement where possible.
